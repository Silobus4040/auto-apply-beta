# Brand colors

Two primary brand colors, both from the RoleRover logo gradient. Use these names when giving direction.

- **Lime** — `#D4FF33` — top of the logo gradient, start of CTA button gradients
- **Teal** — `#00FFCC` — bottom of the logo gradient, defined as `--primary` in most page `<style>` blocks; used for buttons, glows, borders, and accent text throughout the site
